# 8085 vs 8086 vs Modern Processors

| Feature | Intel 8085 (1976) | Intel 8086 (1978) | Modern Processors (2010s–Present) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Capability** | Basic 8-bit processor for simple tasks. | Smarter and faster; handles more information. | Super fast, smart processors (Core i3, i5, i7). |
| **Speed** | Very slow by today's standards. | Faster than the 8085. | Billions of steps per second. |
| **Memory** | Small capacity (like a "small notebook"). | Larger capacity (like a "bigger notebook"). | Huge capacity (like a "full library"). |
| **Applications** | Old machines, calculators, basic control systems. | Early personal computers (e.g., first IBM PCs). | AI, gaming, video, heavy multitasking, energy efficiency. |
